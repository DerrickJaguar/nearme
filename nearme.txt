NearMe Africa — Landing Page & Platform Spec Page 2
1. Purpose & Vision
NearMe Africa is a hyperlocal discovery and commerce platform: businesses list what they sell or offer, and
users nearby find them by geolocation. On top of discovery, a business gets a storefront (powered by Duukaz),
a document toolkit (invoices, receipts, vouchers, flyers), a self-serve ad unit (Tangaza) priced as low as 1,000
UGX per 1,000 impressions, and direct channels for messaging, ratings, and ordering.
The landing page is the front door for two very different audiences at once: consumers searching for something
nearby, and business owners deciding whether to list. The spec below treats the landing page as a dual-funnel
surface and works backward from that into the underlying services engineering needs to build.
Guiding principle: what can we ship that removes real friction between a local business and the customer
standing three streets away from it, today — not a future roadmap item.

3. Core Feature Set
3.1 Geolocation Discovery
● Consumer opens app/web → prompted for location permission (GPS) or manual area/ward entry as fallback.
● Businesses ranked by distance, category relevance, rating, and (if applicable) active Tangaza ad boost.
● Category and keyword search layered on top of proximity (e.g. 'pharmacy', 'boda spare parts').
● Radius control: default 5 km in urban wards, auto-expands in low-density areas until a minimum result count
is met.
3.2 Business Storefront (Duukaz-powered)
● Each listed business gets a storefront page: catalog of products/services, prices, photos, hours, location pin.
● Storefront inventory, pricing, and order management run on the existing Duukaz retail/SME engine —
NearMe is the discovery and traffic layer in front of it, not a second inventory system.
● Storefront supports both 'service' businesses (bookable, no fixed SKU) and 'product' businesses (fixed
catalog, orderable).
3.3 Business Documents
● Invoice and receipt generation tied to storefront orders and offline transactions, branded with the business's
own logo.
● Voucher/coupon generator for promotions, redeemable in-store via code or QR scan.
● Flyer generator: templated promotional graphics a business can post to their own social channels or push as
a Tangaza ad creative directly.
3.4 Tangaza Ad Platform
● Self-serve ad creation from a business's storefront or flyer content — no separate design step required.
● Pricing floor: 1,000 UGX per 1,000 impressions (CPM), with budget cap and daily spend limit set by the
advertiser.
● Ad placements: sponsored position in nearby-search results, category page banners, and (optionally) a
native slot inside the ADT (Africa Daily Times) content feed for cross-portfolio reach.
● Impression and click tracking per ad, feeding a merchant-facing performance dashboard (impressions,
clicks, conversions to storefront visits/orders).
3.5 Messaging, Ratings & Ordering
● In-app chat thread between consumer and business, tied to a specific listing/storefront — not a generic
inbox.
● Star rating plus short review, gated to users who have messaged, ordered, or checked in at the business
(reduces fake reviews).
● Order flow for product businesses: cart → checkout → payment (Tiba Wallet / mobile money) → order status
→ receipt.
● Booking flow for service businesses: request → business confirms/proposes time → calendar hold →
reminder.

6. Technical Architecture (high level)
● Discovery service — geospatial index (e.g. PostGIS or equivalent) over business locations; proximity +
category + rank query.
● Storefront service — thin NearMe layer reading/writing to the existing Duukaz catalog/inventory/order APIs;
no duplicate product data model.
● Document service — templated PDF generation for invoices, receipts, vouchers, and flyers, branded per
business.
● Tangaza ad service — campaign creation, budget/pacing engine, impression & click event pipeline, billing
integration with Tiba Wallet.
● Messaging service — real-time chat (websocket or equivalent) scoped to consumer↔business per listing
thread.
● Ratings service — review submission gated by a verified interaction (order, booking, or message),
aggregate score computation.
● Order/booking service — cart/checkout for product businesses; request/confirm/calendar flow for service
businesses; status webhooks back to messaging.
● Identity & wallet — shared login and payments layer reused across Tiba's portfolio (Tiba Wallet), avoiding a
parallel account system.
Design constraint: NearMe Africa should not re-implement inventory, catalog, or payment logic that already
exists in Duukaz and Tiba Wallet — it is the geolocation discovery, ads, and engagement layer sitting in front of
those systems.

8. Monetization Model
● Tangaza CPM ads — floor rate 1,000 UGX per 1,000 impressions; budget cap and pacing prevent
overspend.
● Featured/boosted listing placement — flat fee or CPM-equivalent for a top-of-results position within a
category or ward.
● Storefront tier — free basic storefront; paid tier for extended catalog size, custom branding, or advanced
document templates.
● Transaction pass-through — optional small fee on in-app orders/bookings settled via Tiba Wallet (aligns
with Duukaz's existing model).

9. Non-Functional Requirements
● Low-bandwidth resilience — landing page and discovery results must degrade gracefully on 2G/3G; image
lazy-load and text-first fallback.
● Multi-market config — country/locale switch for Uganda, Rwanda, South Sudan (currency, language, tax
fields) without a separate codebase.
● Offline-tolerant business tools — invoice/receipt/voucher generation should queue and sync when a
business is briefly offline.
● Geospatial performance — proximity queries must return in under ~300ms at expected listing density per
city.
● Trust & moderation — listing verification workflow and review gating to prevent fake businesses and fake
reviews from the outset.
● Data reuse — no duplicate source of truth for catalog (Duukaz) or payments (Tiba Wallet); NearMe
consumes, does not fork, these.
10. Success Metrics (initial)
● Businesses listed per city/ward within 90 days of launch.
● Search-to-storefront-view conversion rate.
● Storefront-view-to-message-or-order conversion rate.
● Tangaza campaigns created and average CPM spend per active advertiser.
● Review submission rate per completed order/booking.
11. Rollout Plan
Phase Scope
Phase 1 Geolocation discovery + basic listing + storefront read-through from Duukaz, in one city
(Kampala).
Phase 2 Messaging, ratings/reviews, Tangaza self-serve ad creation and billing.
Phase 3 Documents suite (invoices, receipts, vouchers, flyers), order/booking flow, Tiba Wallet payment
integration.
Phase 4 Multi-country expansion (Rwanda, South Sudan), ADT feed ad placement, boosted-listing
auction pricing.
